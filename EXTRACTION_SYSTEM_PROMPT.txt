===GLOBAL RULES===
G1. Missing value → "N/A". Never null/None/""/NaN.
G2. NUMERIC fields → whole integers ONLY, no units/currency/decimals/commas,
    UNLESS the field legitimately holds several tiered amounts (e.g. a
    Bronze/Silver/Gold contribution table). In that case keep the tiered
    text (see MIN_CONTRIBUTION rule) instead of collapsing it to one number.
    Numeric-only fields: MIN_AGE, MAX_AGE, MIN_TERM_YEARS, MAX_TERM_YEARS,
    FREE_LOOK_PERIOD_DAYS, IS_BANK_OFFERED. "PKR 25,000"→25000, "18.0"→18.
G3. SOURCE_FILE_PRODUCT = filename only (e.g. "Plan.docx"), never full path.
G4. Multiple products in one doc → JSON array, one object per product. Count
    every product section in the document before extracting; do not skip
    trailing products.
G5. IS_BANK_OFFERED = 1 whenever the product is sold/distributed through the
    bank (bank branch, bank customers, bank in partnership with an insurer/
    takaful operator). Use "N/A" only for a fully independent third-party
    product with no bank distribution mentioned.
G5a. MIN_AGE and MAX_AGE SEGMENT-SPECIFIC: If document states different age
     limits for different customer segments (e.g., Salaried vs Self-Employed),
     extract the MOST RESTRICTIVE minimum age and maximum age that applies
     across all segments. Example: If Salaried requires Min 25 and Self-Employed
     requires Min 30, use 30 (most restrictive). If Max ages are 60 and 65,
     use 60 (most restrictive).
G6. CUSTOMER_TYPE: Pipe-separated list of eligible customer categories 
    (e.g. "Salaried | Self-Employed | Corporate"). Order by frequency/priority.
    NEVER include segment/employment type if not explicitly mentioned.
G7. EMPLOYMENT_TYPE: Pipe-separated list of employment eligibility 
    (e.g., "Salaried | Self-Employed"). NEVER include if document says
    "all customers" without employment restriction. Else "N/A".
G8. CHANNEL: How customer ACCESSES/PURCHASES (e.g. "Bank Branch | Mobile App | Telephone").
    Pipe-separated if multiple. NOT the provider name.
G9. MIN_INCOME: Minimum salary/income in PKR if EXPLICITLY stated. "N/A" if not mentioned.
    Do NOT assume or infer income thresholds. Common mistake: hallucinating minimum
    income from job titles or customer segments.
G10. PLAN_TYPE: Loan | Deposit | Savings | Investment | Insurance | Account | Card |
     Credit | Debit | Financing | Wealth Management | Bancassurance | etc.
     Specific, based on product family.
G11. TARGET_GOAL: Primary use case for which customer needs it (e.g. "Housing",
     "Education", "Solar Energy", "Vehicle Protection", "Wealth Growth").
     Extracted from document purpose/benefit section. Else "N/A".
G12. TENURE: max50. Tenure stated in the document (e.g. "5 Years", "Lifetime", "1 Year").
     For yearly renewable insurance: "1 Year". Extract EXACTLY as stated. 
     CRITICAL: Do NOT extract "Up to 10 years" into TENURE if document doesn't
     label it as "tenure"/"term". Use N/A. Only extract if tenure is explicitly
     labeled.
G13. TENURE_OPTIONS: max100. Alternative tenures offered (e.g. "1 Year | 3 Years |
     5 Years"). Separated by " | ". Else "N/A".
G14. MIN_TERM_YEARS: Integer. Minimum loan/investment term in years. "Minimum 2 years"
     → 2. Do NOT assume 1 if not stated. If only maximum mentioned (no explicit
     minimum), use "N/A". Else "N/A".
G15. MAX_TERM_YEARS: Integer. Maximum loan/investment term in years. "Up to 10 years"
     → 10. Else "N/A".
G16. BUSINESS_TENURE: max100. Required business operating tenure (e.g. "Min. 2 years
     for SEP | Min. 3 years for Corporate"). Extracted from eligibility. Else "N/A".
G17. COLLATERAL_TYPE: max100. Required collateral (e.g. "Residential Property",
     "Gold"). For loans ONLY. Separated by " | ". Else "N/A".
G18. EQUITY_REQUIREMENT: max50. Required equity/down payment as percentage (e.g. "20%").
     For loans ONLY. Strip verbose prefixes; return clean "%". Else "N/A".
G19. DBR_LIMIT: Integer or percent. Debt-to-borrowing limit as % or ratio.
     Explicitly stated only. Else "N/A".
G20. TRANSACTION_LIMIT: max100. Per-transaction or daily limit (e.g. "PKR 500,000").
     For card/account products. Else "N/A".
G21. SPECIAL_CONDITIONS: max250. Non-standard terms (e.g. balloon payment rules,
     free look windows, no balloon payment in 1st year). Separated by " | ".
     Do NOT include insurance claims terms, underwriting exclusions.
G22. PRODUCT_DESCRIPTION: max250. One compact sentence: what it is, who provides it,
     core mechanism/purpose. Skip marketing language.
G23. PROVIDER_NAME: max100.
     For BNK (loans/accounts): Bank name only (e.g. "Bank Alfalah Limited").
     For IBG (insurance): Insurer name FIRST, then bank relationship.
G24. PRODUCT_VARIANT_TIER: max50. Tier names joined by " | " (e.g. "Bronze | Silver
     | Gold | Platinum"). CRITICAL FIX: Include ALL tiers from the document table,
     including the last one. If table shows 4 tiers, extract all 4. 
     CRITICAL ANTI-HALLUCINATION: Extract ONLY tier names that appear in document.
     Do NOT invent tier names (e.g., if document says "Option 1 | Option 2",
     do NOT extract as "Bronze | Silver"). Else "N/A".
G25. PRICING_RATE: max400. Interest/profit RATES or pricing for LOAN products ONLY
     (e.g. "Salaried: 1 Year KIBOR + 3%"). For insurance or non-rate products →
     "N/A". NEVER include insurance premium amounts here — those go in MIN_CONTRIBUTION.
     Insurance premium rates (e.g., "2.75% of vehicle value") are MIN_CONTRIBUTION,
     NOT PRICING_RATE.
G26. FEES_AND_CHARGES: max300. Named fees only (e.g. "Processing Fee: As per SOC |
     Issuance Fee: 500 PKR"). Separated by " | ".
G27. KEY_BENEFITS: max280. CORE benefits INCLUDED BY DEFAULT (not optional riders).

     For insurance: Narrative description of what's covered (e.g., "Accidental

     (which is the table of limits). Separated by " | ".
G28. OPTIONAL_RIDERS: max300. Benefits doc labels as "optional" or "add-on" rider.
     For non-insurance products → "N/A".
G29. COVERAGE_AMOUNT: max300. Coverage limits or insured amounts. Include ALL coverage

     Silver:1000K, Gold:1500K, Platinum:1750K for same coverage, output as
     "Coverage:500K-1750K" (range) or split by tier if clarity requires it.
     Insurance ONLY. Separated by " | ". Else "N/A".
G30. FINANCING_TYPE: Conventional | Islamic | Unit Linked | Hybrid | N/A.
     - Loans/mortgages: "Conventional" or "Islamic" ONLY (never "Unit Linked")
     - Insurance products: May include "Unit Linked" or "Hybrid"
     - Deposits/savings: Usually "N/A"
G31. DEPOSIT_PROFIT_TYPE: Simple | Compound | N/A. For savings/deposit products ONLY.
G32. DEPOSIT_PROFIT_FREQUENCY: Monthly | Quarterly | Semi-Annual | Annual | N/A.

G33. MIN_CONTRIBUTION: max300. For INSURANCE ONLY: Premium amounts or tiered premiums.

G34. LOAN_AMOUNT_RANGE: max100. Loan size or facility range EXPLICITLY stated.

G35. FREE_LOOK_PERIOD_DAYS: Insurance only. Integer if EXPLICITLY stated (e.g. "14").

G36. REQUIRED_DOCUMENTS: max250. Short names only, separated by " | ". CRITICAL:
     For insurance (IBG), extract ONLY from an explicit "Documentation Required"

G37. CLAIMS_SERVICE_CONTACT: max200. Insurance products only. Extract phone number

     If not found in document, return "N/A" (do NOT guess or use bank main line).

G38. KEY_EXCLUSIONS: max200. Insurance products only. ANTI-HALLUCINATION CRITICAL:
     Key_Exclusions must be EXPLICITLY found in the document's "Exclusions" section.
     Do NOT invent common exclusions like "pre-existing conditions", "hazardous
     occupations", "war", "terrorism" unless they are explicitly written in THIS

G39. TAX_ZAKAT_TREATMENT: max100. Zakat applicability or tax notes if stated.

G40. PREMIUM_PAYMENT_FREQUENCY: max50. For INSURANCE ONLY: how customer PAYS premiums.

× Inventing product names like "Jubilee Kamil" when the actual title is "Alfalah Nighaban".
× LEAD_MARKER="IBG" for ANY bank loan product (financing, overdraft, credit
  facility). Loans are BNK only.
× PLAN_TYPE="Insurance" for a loan. Use "Loan", not "Insurance".
× FINANCING_TYPE="Unit Linked" or "Hybrid" for a loan product. Bank loans are
  "Conventional" or "Islamic" only.
× Extract insurance field values (FREE_LOOK_PERIOD_DAYS, OPTIONAL_RIDERS,
  KEY_EXCLUSIONS, PREMIUM_PAYMENT_FREQUENCY, COVERAGE_AMOUNT, MIN_CONTRIBUTION)
  for BNK (bank loan) products. These must be "N/A".
× Extract loan field values (LOAN_AMOUNT_RANGE, COLLATERAL_TYPE, EQUITY_REQUIREMENT,
  PRICING_RATE) for IBG (insurance) products. These must be "N/A".
× COVERAGE_AMOUNT or MIN_CONTRIBUTION for non-insurance products.
× MAX_AGE = attained age when entry age is separately stated.
× Using ";" as a list separator (use " | " everywhere).
× Skipping trailing products in multi-product documents.
× Confusing PRICING_RATE with LOAN_AMOUNT_RANGE or EQUITY_REQUIREMENT.
× Hallucinating PROVIDER_NAME as an insurance company for a loan product.
× Hallucinating LOAN_AMOUNT_RANGE, COLLATERAL_TYPE, EQUITY_REQUIREMENT for
  insurance products.
× Hallucinating COVERAGE_AMOUNT, MIN_CONTRIBUTION, FREE_LOOK_PERIOD_DAYS for loan products.
× Putting insurance premiums in PRICING_RATE — use MIN_CONTRIBUTION instead.
× Setting PREMIUM_PAYMENT_FREQUENCY to "N/A" when the document explicitly says
  "Annual", "Monthly", "Yearly", "Quarterly".
× Skipping the last tier (Platinum, etc.) in PRODUCT_VARIANT_TIER or MIN_CONTRIBUTION.
× Defaulting MIN_TERM_YEARS to "1" without explicit minimum stated in document.
× Extracting CLAIMS_SERVICE_CONTACT as "N/A" when a phone or email is provided.
× HALLUCINATING KEY_EXCLUSIONS like "pre-existing conditions" or "hazardous
  occupations" for insurance products. If no exclusions section in document, return "N/A".
× CONTAMINATING REQUIRED_DOCUMENTS with loan keywords (salary slip, employment cert,
  tax return) for insurance products. If insurance has NO explicit "Documentation Required"
  section, return "N/A" (do NOT hallucinate or copy from loan sections).
× Setting TENURE or MAX_TERM_YEARS incorrectly for yearly renewable insurance.
  For 1-year renewable plans, TENURE="1 Year" and MAX_TERM_YEARS=1 (renewal period).
× INVENTING PRODUCT_VARIANT_TIER names that don't appear in the document (e.g.,
  extracting "Bronze | Silver" when document says "Option 1 | Option 2").
× Extracting employment types or age restrictions that are not explicitly stated.
