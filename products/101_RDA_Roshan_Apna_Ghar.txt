RDA – Roshan Apna Ghar
Product Features
Bank Alfalah Islamic offer Roshan Apna Ghar (RAG) House Finance facility to all non-
resident customers of Roshan Digital Account (RDA). This facility will be available for all
Bank Alfalah RDA account holder customers who are either willing to keep their RDA
balance/INPC investment as lien against their net financing amount as collateral or avail
financing without cash collateral.
