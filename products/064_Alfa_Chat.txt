Product Name and brief description
‘Ask Alfa’ services will be available within AlfaChat > Alfa App where customers can
communicate their queries and grievances as well as share files & documents to update
their account credentials and any requests as per BAFL (Bank Alfalah) requirements
pertaining to the customer

FAQs
Q1) what is the purpose this message? / Why did I received this message on update of
information?
ANS: As part of bank’s policy on updating the client information, you have received this
message. To better serve the client for future banking relation we request to fill the
form accordingly.

Q2) what sort of information does the bank require?
ANS t: Bank Alfalah’s account holders are required to fill the Know Your Customer (KYC)
form which has been designed for your ease and upload necessary document.
Q3) I am a salaried person by profession. What documents do you require?
ANS: A salaried person can upload one of the following document:

Salary slip not older than 3 months

Valid Job Card

A Valid contract signed between the employer and employee

Employee confirmation letter from the employer not older than 3 months
Q4) I am business individual by profession. What documents do you require?
ANS: Please confirm what sort of business do you operate?

Sole proprietorship

Partnership Entity

Director & Shareholder of Private Limited Entity

Director & Shareholder of Public Limited Entity

Director & Shareholder of SMC Private Limited Entity

Self Employed professional (Example; Lawyer/Advocate, Freelancer, Real Estate Agent,
etc.)
Q5) I am operating a Sole proprietor business.
ANS: Kindly upload the following documents:

Sole proprietorship declaration on business letterhead

Online NTN verification
Q6) I am operating a Partnership Entity.
ANS: Kindly upload the following documents:
147.
Partnership Deed
148.
Partnership Registration Document
149.
Note: In-case Partnership Deed is not registered; the same to be notarized

Q7) I am Director and Shareholder of the Private Limited Entity.
ANS: Kindly upload one of the following documents:
180.
Latest Form 29 and Form A attested by SECP
181.
Confirmation of Directorship in the entity signed by Company Secretary
182.
Note: In case the updated Form 29 and Form A are not updated; the prior one
should bear ‘Certified True Copy’ attestation by SECP of the current year.
Q8) I am Director and Shareholder of the Public Limited Entity.
ANS: Kindly upload one of the following documents:
127.
Updated Form 29 and Form A attested by SECP
128.
Confirmation of Directorship in the entity signed by Company Secretary
129.
Note: In case the updated Form 29 and Form A are not updated; the prior
one should bear ‘Certified True Copy’ attestation by SECP of the current year.

Q13) I am a housewife/household and financially supported by my
husband/son/sister/daughter/relative.
ANS: Kindly upload the following documents:
Self-undertaking on plain paper declaring the profession and details of fund
provider/supporter
Fund provider/supporter’s ID Document and source of income document
Note: In case the housewife is self-dependent on her investments and savings; relevant
proof of funds required (Saving certificates, bank statement, CDC statement
(investment in stocks), dividend warrants)
Q14) I am a student and financially supported by my father/mother/sibling/relative.
ANS: Kindly upload the following documents:
Self-undertaking on plain paper declaring the profession and details of fund
provider/supporter
Fund provider/supporter’s ID Document and source of income document
Valid Student proof
Note: In case the student is dependent upon scholarship or self-savings; relevant proof
of funds required (Scholarship document from competent authority, bank statement)
Q15) I am unemployed and financially supported by my
father/mother/spouse/sibling/children/relative.
ANS: Kindly upload the following documents:
Self-undertaking on plain paper declaring the profession and details of fund
provider/supporter
Fund provider/supporter’s ID Document and source of income document
Note: In case the unemployed is self-dependent on his/her investments and savings;
relevant proof of funds required (Saving certificates, bank statement, CDC statement

Q16) I am a retired person and financially dependent upon pension.
ANS: Kindly upload the following documents:

Self-undertaking on plain paper declaring the profession and details of income
Retirement evidence / pension document / end of service letter
Q17) I am a landlord / landlady.
ANS: Kindly upload the following documents:
Fard endorsed by local Mukhtiyarkar bearing the ownership of land
Valid Rental Agreement(s
