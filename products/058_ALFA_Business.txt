partnered scheme, you can accept customer payments via scanning the QR.

FAQs
Does the customer bear any additional charge as they pay via scanning QR
code at Alfa Business App?
Absolutely not! Customers aren’t charged any additional amount for paying via scanning
QR at Alfa Business App
Who can pay via Alfa Business QR?
Any customer whose banking/payment App can read QR codes generated by Bank
Alfalah-partnered payments scheme (e.g. Mastercard and Visa)
Whom should I contact in case of a dispute?
Contact Bank Alfalah help-line 021-111-225-111 and lock your dispute.
Where will I receive my settled amount?
You can receive your settled amount in Alfa wallet, Bank Alfalah account, Cheque or
any other bank account. However, for best rates, choose Alfa Wallet or Alfalah Bank
Account as your settlement mode.
What to do if I forget my Username or password?
Your username is your Alfa registered business name. To reset password, login Alfa
Business App, click ‘Forget Password’, enter your ‘user name’, verify OTP and easily
reset your password.
