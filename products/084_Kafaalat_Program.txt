Kafaalat Program
Product Features
Kafaalat- Unconditional cash transfer program. It comes under G2P Payments
BAFL provides digital payment solutions for cash disbursements to beneficiaries through
biometric system in Khyber Pakhtoon Khuwa, Azad Jammu & Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan
Product Features/ Benefits:
Monthly disbursements of PKR 2,000 provided to the poorest strata of the society through
agent network, ATMs/CDMs and branches
Target Market
Kafaalat beneficiaries
Documentation Required
Original CNIC
Registration Process
Registered as per data provided by Kafaalat program
