IGI LIFE WTO Uroos Marriage Plan
IGI Life – WTO Uroos - Marriage Plan (Underwritten by IGI Life Insurance) is a saving
and protection plan to assist customers in planning for their children’ education. It is
only available through Bank Alfalah’s branches.
