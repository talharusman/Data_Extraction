Alfalah Islamic Current Account
Product Features
Alfalah Islamic Current Account works in accordance with Shari’ah principles. And it can
be opened with just Rs. 1000. Falah Islamic Current account is a basic non-profit
bearing checking account based on the concept of Qardh, providing an opportunity for
individuals, business and other institutions to avail frequent transaction privileges,
unlimited deposits & withdrawals ,funds accessibility and online banking across the
country with BAFL`s wide network of branches.
Product Feature / Benefits / Unique Selling Points:

First free cheque Books

Account statements (semiannually)

E-statements

Online transactions within the Bank Alfalah network
Target Market
Corporate entities, SMEs, retail business, salaried individuals, NGOs & clubs have the
need to conduct transactions frequently across the country. Falah Islamic current
account caters to the banking needs of individuals, business segment and other
institutions which are interest averse and need to conduct frequent transactions and
require fund accessibility. However, Institutions, Corporations and Firms whose
businesses are not Shari’ah compliant are not eligible to open Falah Islamic Current
Account.
