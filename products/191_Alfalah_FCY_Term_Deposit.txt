Alfalah FCY Term Deposit
Product Features
FCY Term Deposit offers attractive investment options in foreign currency with tenors
starting from 1 month up to 1 year.
Product Feature / Benefits:

FCY Term Deposit is a fixed deposit, tier-based product.

Amount can be placed for 1 month, 3 months, 6 months and 1 year

Profit is paid at maturity.

No minimum placement amount.

For individuals, joint account holders, sole proprietors, private limited , public limited
companies & partnerships
Unique Selling Points:

Attractive rate of profit.

Flexible tenors ranging from 1 month to 1 year.

All Segments

FAQs
Q1: Do all BAL branches country-wide offer this product?
A: No. Only branches with FCY licenses can offer FCY deposit product.
Q2: Who can place deposits in this account?
A: This account is for individuals/joint account holders, sole proprietors, private limited
companies, public limited companies, partnerships etc
Q3: Can the customer withdraw the deposit before the end of the fixed
deposit period?
A: Yes. However, a premature encashment penalty will be levied as per the bank’s

schedule of charges. The cases mentioned above illustrate the calculation of premature
encashment penalty amount (if applicable).
Q4: How often will the profit be credited?
A: The profit will be credited on maturity
Q5: Is automatic rollover facility available for this term deposit?
A: Yes, automatic rollover facility is available for this term deposit.
