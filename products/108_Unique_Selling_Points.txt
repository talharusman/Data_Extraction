Alfalah Islamic Business Way Deposit and Payroll Remunerative Current Account
Product Features
Alfalah Islamic Business Way (Remunerative Current Account) can be opened as a
deposit or a payroll account. The deposit account can be opened with an initial deposit
of PKR 1000/- only while for a payroll account, there is no need of initial deposit and
balance requirement. Profit calculation is on average monthly balance and profit
disbursement is on monthly basis.
