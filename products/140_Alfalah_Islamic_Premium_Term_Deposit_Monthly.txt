Alfalah Islamic Premium Term Deposit- Monthly
Nature of Product
A profit bearing term deposit based on the
principles of Mudarabah, where Customer is
“Rab-ul-Maal” and Bank is “Mudarib”. Bank
may, at its discretion, use or employ such funds
as it deems fit under the products and avenues
approved by the Shariah Board of the Bank.
Bank shall share the profit on the basis of
predetermined profit sharing ratio. Return on
deposits will be based on predetermined
weightages for each month or any interval
decided by the bank and announced as per
SBP’s instructions. A sheet containing Profit
Sharing Ratio and Weightages information shall
be available on Customer’s demand and also be
placed at the public notice board of branches
and on the website of the bank.
In the event of loss to the pool, all the investors
of the pool shall share such loss proportionate
to their investment. The Bank may at its option
also participate in Mudarabah Pool as “Rab-ul-
Maal” by comingling its own equity/funds and
the funds of other depositors including the
current deposits as and when required.
