Khayaal Rakhna Account
Product Features
Khayaal Rakhna Account works in accordance with Shari’ah principles and it can be
opened with just Rs. 100/- only.
Product Feature / Benefits / Unique Selling Points:

Minimum initial deposit requirement of account opening is Rs 100 only*

No minimum and maximum balance limit for profit eligibility

Profit is calculated on average balance of the month and paid semi-annually

No restriction on number of deposit or withdrawal transactions

Single tier account
Target Market
The target market for Khayaal Rakhna Account is individuals, sole proprietors, traders, NGOs,
business entities etc.
Eligibility Criteria

Meeting eligibility & KYC criteria as per Bank’s guidelines.

Documents requirement as per normal checking account opening for Individuals / Sole
proprietors / Company / Partnership / Proprietor / NGOs / Clubs / Traders etc.

Institutions, Corporations and Firms whose businesses are not Shari’ah compliant are not
eligible
