Alfalah Islamic Asaan Saving Account
Product Features
Alfalah Islamic Asaan savings account works in accordance with Shari’ah principles.
Initial deposit for savings account is just Rs. 100/- only.
Product Feature / Benefits / Unique Selling Points:

No Minimum balance requirement

Avail a 6-monthly profit rate on your Alfalah Islamic Asaan Savings account

Free Online Banking through any Alfalah Islamic Branch

Monthly cash deposit/withdrawal limit of up to Rs. 1 million
*As per “BPRD/AML-01/2023-2280 - Temporary Enhancement of Limits of Asaan
Account for Facilitation of Intending Pilgrims of Hajj 2023, In order to facilitate
intending pilgrims of Hajj 2023, limits for Asaan Accounts are temporarily enhanced
from PKR 1 million to PKR 1.5 million. The enhanced limits shall be valid till June 30,
2023.
Target Market
The Asaan Account is targeted at common people and is open to all low income
unbanked/under-banked masses that face difficulties in account opening due to normal
account opening requirements or lesser means. These segments of society may include
but are not limited to skilled/unskilled work force, farmers, less educated/uneducated
people, laborers/daily wagers, women/housewives, self-employed individuals,
pensioners, young adult population etc.
Only individuals can open Alfalah Islamic Asaan Saving Account.
Eligibility Criteria

Meeting eligibility & KYC criteria as per Bank’s guidelines

Documents requirement as per normal checking account opening for Individuals.
