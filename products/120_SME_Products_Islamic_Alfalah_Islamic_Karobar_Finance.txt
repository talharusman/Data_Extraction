SME Products (Islamic) Alfalah Islamic Karobar Finance
Product Overview:
Alfalah Islamic Karobar Finance Product has been structured specially to cater
needs of Small & medium businesses. The finance under the "Alfalah Islamic
Karobar Finance" shall be provided as a bundle offer wherein different facilities i.e.
Murabahah, Istisna, Tijarah, Letter of Credit, Export Facilities, Letter of Guarantee
will be offered as independent limits or as sub limits/interchangeable limits
depending on customer need keeping in view the collateral constrains.
Underlying Islamic Mode:
Murabahah, Istisna, Tijarah, Letter of Credit, Export Facilities, Letter of Guarantee
Type Of Product:
SME/Commercial
Target Customer:
Small & medium business enterprises
Basis for Pricing Returns:
KIBOR + Spread
Minimum & Maximum Financing Limit:
Minimum amount of Finance to a customer shall be Rs0.500 million and maximum
limit up to Rs 30 million (25 million for SE) or as revised and approved from time
to time by the bank
Tenors (Minimum & Maximum)
Minimum period of 1 Year
