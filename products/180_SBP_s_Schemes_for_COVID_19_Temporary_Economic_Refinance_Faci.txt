SBP's Schemes for COVID-19 Temporary Economic Refinance Facility
Product Feature
In order to support sustainable economic growth especially in the backdrop of
challenges being faced by the industry in post-pandemic scenario, State Bank of
Pakistan (SBP) has introduced Temporary Economic Refinance Facility (TERF). It is a
long term finance facility for purchase of new imported and locally manufactured plant
& machinery for setting of new projects.
Product Feature/ Benefits:

The facility will provide concessionary refinance for setting up of new industrial units.

Under this scheme, maximum financing limit is of Rs. 5 billion per project.

As for the validity of the facility, LCs/ILCs established from the date of announcement
of the scheme till 31st March, 2021 will be eligible for the TERF.

Financing is available for a maximum of 10 years including grace period up to 2 years.

Maximum mark-up rate of 7% p.a. (SBP rate of refinance will be 3%)

FAQs
What is the maximum financing limit under TERF?
The maximum financing limit under TERF is of 5 Billion.
When will the facility expire?
The Facility will expire on March 31, 2021. All LCs established after announcement of the
Facility till March 31, 2021 shall be eligible for financing.
What will be the maximum period of financing?
Financing shall be available for a maximum period of 10 years including a maximum grace
period of 2 years.
