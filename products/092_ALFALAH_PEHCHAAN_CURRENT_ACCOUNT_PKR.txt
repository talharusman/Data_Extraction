FAQs
Q1: Does all BAFL branches country-wide offer this product?
A: Yes – all BAFL conventional branches offer this product.
Q2: Who can open this account?
A: This account can only be opened by females only.
Q3: Can NRPs open this account?
A: Yes, NRP women can open this account on the basis of their identity proof.
Q4: What is the minimum balance requirement for this account?
A: There is no minimum balance requirement for this account.

Q5: When will account statement be generated?
A: Account statement will be generated on semi-annual basis.
Q6: Will customer be issued a cheque book and VISA Debit/ATM card?
A: Yes, the customer will get free customized cheque book. VISA Debit/ATM card can be issued,
however, charges may be applicable as per the prevailing Schedule of Charges (SOC).
Q7: Can I open a joint account on this product?
A: No, joint accounts cannot be opened on this product.
Q8: What is the minimum requirement for waiver on lockers?
A: Customers maintaining PKR accounts may get waivers on locker rental subject to maintaining
an average balance of PKR 500,000/-, in their PKR accounts. This is subject to availability of
lockers in the branch.
Note: Bank Alfalah is currently offering Free Cheque book Issuance, Pay Order Issuance,
Online Banking Transactions & Cheque clearing Services (including Same-Day & Intercity
Clearing) across the board for all its customers without any balance requirement or conditions.
