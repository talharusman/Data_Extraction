EOBI
Product Features
EOBI (Employee Old Age Benefit Institute) had given the mandate to bank Alfalah to
digitalize their pension and contribution process
Product Features/ Benefits:
EOBI Pension Account:

Account Opening

Bio-matric Proof of Life

ATM cash withdrawal

Mobile banking (Alfa)

UBP Payment

Mobile top-up
Contribution Collection:

Collection at branches

Collection via internet banking
Please note: EOBI wallet holders will not be able to perform IBFT.
Associated Charges
EOBI Pension Account:
No fee is charged to the Pensioner, however if the pensioner opt for the services of
Mobile banking (Alfa) then the charges will be charged as per mobile banking SOC.
Contribution Collection:
No charges Levied to customer for distribution or contribution collection.
