Islamic Alfalah Green Energy
Product Features
Alfalah Islamic Green Finance Solution – is a Consumer Finance facility for customers
willing to install Solar Energy Solution to generate electricity for residential purposes on
customer owned house.
This financing solution aligns with the principles of Islamic financing (Diminishing
Musharakah - Shirkat ul Milk).
