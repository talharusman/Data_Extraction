Falah Senior Citizens Savings Account
Product Features
Falah Senior Citizens Savings Account is a Mudarabah based product which provides
senior citizens with financial safety and independence so that they can spend their
retirement in peace.
Product Features/ Benefits/ Unique Selling Points:

Free SMS alerts

Free unlimited cheque books

Free ATM/Debit Card (Only Classic, Gold or PayPak with no issuance or annual charges)

Free Banker’s Cheque (Issuance, Duplicate issuance and cancellation)

No maximum or minimum balance limits

Free Online Transaction Nationwide

Unlimited number of withdrawals and deposits

Profit calculation on daily balance basis and monthly profit disbursement

Free six monthly statement of account in hard copy (if e-statement has not been opted)

Free monthly e‐Statements

Falah Senior Citizens Privilege Certificate shall be given to all Falah Senior Citizens
Account holders which shall allow the customers to have a priority branch experience
and to avail fastened services at branches such as fast tracked utility bill payments,
cash withdrawals and deposits etc.
Terms and Conditions:

All charges will be applicable as per the prevailing SOBC

Profit on Falah Senior Citizens Account will be decided as per Pool Management
guidelines.

Zakat and WHT to be deducted as per prevailing rules and regulations.

The product is based on the concept of Mudarabah.
Target Market
The target market for the product are senior citizens aged 55 or above that are looking
for high halal returns on their life long savings. They can be old aged civilians, retired
military officials, pensioners, soon to be pensioners, etc.
Eligibility Criteria
Pakistani National with CNIC/NICOP/SNIC aged 55 and above Individuals and joint
accountholders. In case of joint applicants, the primary applicant should be at least 55

years of age or above, and the other joint account holder must be at least 18 years of
age.
