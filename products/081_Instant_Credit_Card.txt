Eligibility Criteria
-
Customer should have at least 1-year branch relationship (only individual
account) – Conventional/Islamic
-
Maintaining minimum average balance of PKR 50,000 for last 6 months in his
account (Conventional)
-
CASA account holders only (Conventional)
-
Must not be default / Delinquent / Overdue in any facility
-
Account should not be dormant / Inactive
-
Account category High Risk / Politically Exposed may apply through conventional
application instead ALFA app
-
Must meet Debt Burden Requirement of his income to liability ratio
