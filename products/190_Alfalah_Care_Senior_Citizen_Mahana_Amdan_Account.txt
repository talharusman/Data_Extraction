Alfalah Care Senior Citizen Mahana Amdan Account
Product Features
Alfalah Senior Citizen Mahana Amdan Account is a term deposit account which gives
customers aged 55 and above, a high rate of returns on fixed deposits with the benefit
of monthly profits.
Product Features/ Benefits:

Alfalah Mahana Amdan is a 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 year fixed term deposit

Profit is paid monthly

Minimum placement amount of PKR 100,000

No upper limit on deposit amount

Only for individual/joint account holders and customers.
Companies/corporates/businesses NOT eligible for this product

Deposit in PKR only
Unique Selling Points:

Attractive rate of profit

Profit calculated on the total fixed deposit and credited monthly

Auto renewal facility.
Target Market
Customers aged 55 and above
Eligibility Criteria
Only for individual/joint account holders and customers.
Companies/corporates/businesses NOT eligible for this product
FAQs
Q1: Do all BAL branches country-wide offer this product?
A: Yes – all BAFL conventional branches offer this product.
Q2: Who can place deposits in this account?
A: This Product can be availed by Pensioners, soon to be Pensioners, Retired Military
Officials and individuals aged 55 years or more. This product is NOT available for
companies/corporate/businesses.

Q3: What is the minimum and maximum tenor of Term Deposit customer can
book?
A: Customer can book a Term Deposit for a minimum period of 1 year ranging to a
maximum period of 5 years.
Q4: What is the minimum amount of Term Deposit I can book?
A: Customer can book a Term Deposit for a minimum a minimum amount of PKR
100,000/-.
Q5: How often will the profit be credited?
A: The profit will be credited on a monthly basis.
Q6: Is automatic rollover facility available for this term deposit?
A: Yes, automatic rollover facility is available for this term deposit.
