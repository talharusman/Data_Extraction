Alfalah Islamic Musharakah Savings Account
Product Features
Alfalah Musharaka Saving Account not only gives return on investments, but also makes
day to day business banking needs extremely convenient with a host of free banking
services.
Alfalah Musharaka Saving Account is a Mudarabah based product that would deliver
attractive profit rates to its medium and large sized depositors. This account not only
gives return on investments, but also makes day to day business banking needs
extremely convenient. It is a tier based product offering higher profit on higher
balances with no transaction limits and convenience of fund accessibility.
Product Feature / Benefits / Unique Selling Points:

Profit is calculated on average balance of the month and paid every month

Minimum initial deposit requirement for account opening is Rs. 100 only

No minimum and maximum balance limit for profit eligibility

No restriction on number of deposit or withdrawal transactions

Multiple tiers

Free first cheque book

Complimentary account statement semi-annually

Free E-statements
Target Market
The target market for Alfalah Musharaka Saving Account is Sole proprietors, Medium
and Large sized traders and self-employed individuals except Institutions, Corporations
and Firms whose businesses are not Shari’ah compliant.
Eligibility Criteria

Meeting eligibility & KYC criteria as per Bank’s guidelines.

Documents requirement as per normal checking account opening for Individuals / Sole
proprietors / Company / Partnership / Proprietor / NGOs / Clubs / Traders etc.

Institutions, Corporations and Firms whose businesses are not Shari’ah compliant are
not eligible.
