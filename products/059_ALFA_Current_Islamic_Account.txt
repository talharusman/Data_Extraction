Registration Process
Subscription Process:

In the Alfa application, customer shall select ‘Open Alfa Account’ (to access the
registration menu)

A pop-up will appear asking the customer to select whether he/she wants to open ‘Alfa
Islamic’ or ‘Alfa Conventional’ account

Select Alfa Islamic option

Select Transact option

Enter the following information

CNIC No

CNIC Issuance Date

Mobile Number

OTP will be sent to customer’s registered mobile number

Enter OTP and create PIN and Password

Enter the following additional information

Place of Birth

City of Stay

Mother’s Name

Father’s Name

Upload/capture CNIC front and back

Alfa Current Islamic Account Level 0 opened successfully

SMS will be sent to customer’s registered mobile number

Customer will be informed to visit BAFL branch for Biometric Verification & Upgrade
(Level 1) to Alfa Current Islamic Account
