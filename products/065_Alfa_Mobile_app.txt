FAQs
Who is eligible to use Alfa?
All Alfalah Active Account / Debit or Credit Card customers can register. Alfalah App
customers can also use their existing User ID & Password to login Alfa. In-case you do

not have an Alfalah Account or a Credit Card please request via ALFA or call us at +92
21 111-225-224.
Which Username and Password do I use?
You can use your existing Alfalah Internet Banking username and password to log in. If
you do not have an Internet Banking account, you can register instantly by
downloading ALFA.
What is meant by what is meant by “No transactional limit on Own BAFL
Credit Card bill payment”?
There is a per day transactional limit on Alfa & IB, which will not decrease, if a
customer who is registered on Alfa/IB pays his/her own BAFL credit card (issued to
same CNIC).
Do I need a specific mobile connection for this service?
No. This service will work on any type of Internet connection. It could be through a Wi-
Fi connection or your pre-paid or post-paid mobile network connection.
What do I require to use ALFA?
You need the following to use Alfa Mobile App: - Any Android or Apple (iOS)
smartphone - Active Alfalah Account / Debit or Credit Card, registered mobile number &
email address - Username - Password
Are there any other charges?
IBFT service charge is PKR 50+ FED per transaction.
What is the bank charging for this service?
Alfa is Free for customers who are already using Alfalah Internet or App. However there
is an annual charge for Alfa as per your transaction limits package.
Where can I download Alfa from?
ALFA can be downloaded from Apple Store or Google Play Store on your smartphone.
Can I use Alfa if I am travelling outside Pakistan?
Yes you may use Alfa from anywhere. All you need is a stable Internet connection.
What if I forget my or Password?
You can reset your password through Alfa or call Alfa helpline at +92 21 111-225-224
to reset your password.
How do I subscribe for Alfa?
You do not need to sign any physical form or visit Alfalah branch to register for ALFA. -

Download Alfa on your smartphone - Register using Account, Debit or Credit Card &
CNIC information - If you require guidance please contact us at +92 21 111-225-224.
If my Alfalah debit card is blocked, does my Alfa service gets blocked as
well? No, your Alfa will work.
Is my per day transaction limit shared?
Yes, your per day transaction limit is shared. It gets reduced if you perform any
transaction on App.
Is this service available 24 hours?
Yes. This service is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
What is AlfaPay?
AlfaPay is a payment tool that lets you make payments by scanning QR code at
different merchants. It is a new digital way of making purchases and merchant
payments using any of your Bank Alfalah Accounts, Credit Card, Wallets & Orbits.
What are the AlfaPay Limits?
Each transaction limit of AlfaPay is PKR 10,000 however the daily limit is as per your
Alfa package.
Are there any additional fees when using AlfaPay?
There are no additional fees on AlfaPay.
Where can I use AlfaPay?
You can use AlfaPay wherever you see the Masterpass QR button/sticker. You can also
click here to see a list of outlets.
What is Phone Tagging?
AlfaPay lets you tag your phone for instant payments with out the need to login. Your
default payment option will be automatically selected
What is AlfaPay PIN?
If your authorization mode is AlfaPay PIN then you would need a 4 digit pin to perform
AlfaPay transactions prior to login.
What should I do if my mobile device is lost or stolen?
Your password or AlfaPay PIN is required to authorize any transaction using your
smartphone. You can also deactivate your phone tagging by signing in from another
device
Where can I find my AlfaPay transaction history?
You can view transaction details on AlfaPay for up to 30 days.

Is user name case sensitive on both Alfa & IB?
Username is case sensitive on Alfa but not on IB
What if customer forgets the username?
'Forget Username’ option is available for customer facility if customer has forgotten the
security question also then please guide customer to call our Call Centre at 111-225-
111 for assistance.
Can customer change his/her User ID/Password?
You cannot change your User ID, however you may change your password through the
'Change Password' option available in IB and from ‘My Alfa App’ in Alfa. If you opted to
un-subscribe from Internet Banking/Alfa, only then you set a new User ID.
If I have registered as a debit card holder and later I block my Debit Card
will my Internet Banking will get blocked too?
No! Your Internet Banking/Alfa will be active regardless your debit card status.
What account types are allowed to register on Alfa/IB?
All BAFL checking account holders including single, joint (with ‘singly’ or, ‘either or
survivor’ or ‘Any one of us’, operating instructions), sole proprietorship are eligible for
internet banking/ALFA facility whether they hold debit card or not.
